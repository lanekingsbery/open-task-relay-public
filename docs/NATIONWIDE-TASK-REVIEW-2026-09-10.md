# Nationwide public-good task review — September 10, 2026 UTC

Prepublication editorial review: **52 tasks, four in each of the 13 existing categories; one per U.S. state plus Guam and Puerto Rico (52 states/territories).** No jurisdiction receives a second task. Alaska, Hawaii, the Mountain West, Plains, Midwest, South, Northeast, Southwest, California, Pacific Northwest, Pacific territories and Caribbean are represented. Localities include suburbs, regional centers, smaller communities, rural study areas and a few major metros. The places are named for context, not characterized as deficient.

Each brief identifies a beneficiary, bounded output, public starting sources, evidence requirements, allowed uncertainty and an explicit next check. The stored leg budget is five minutes; a single checked field or record is useful in roughly 30 seconds. Estimated full artifacts are 20 minutes spread over independent legs. No results or participation are manufactured.

## Inventory and editorial checks

The public `/api/v1/tasks` inventory was read on September 10, 2026 UTC using limit 100, offsets 0 and 100, and the terminal pagination marker; an additional offset-200 read was empty. It contained **174 visible tasks: 170 open, two completed, two closed**. All 170 open titles and descriptions were considered, across categories. Local prior-release definitions were also checked for ID and title collisions. A full-pair vocabulary comparison flagged candidates for editorial inspection; it was not treated as proof of semantic uniqueness. The inventory will be refreshed immediately before merge.

The final batch has distinct subjects and deliverables within each category. Examples of reviewed neighboring topics: Kenai passage ratings concern habitat-survey evidence, not the existing Montana bridge-condition task; Indiana transfer agreements concern completed college credits, not the existing work-experience-credit assessment; Roanoke raster NoData and vertical units differ from Dover parcel/zoning portal metadata; Chicago drawing dimensions differ from indexing Appalachian school holdings; Puerto Rico bilingual machine-field documentation differs from the existing single-station text graph companion.

Within the new batch, RIPTA visitor certification is distinct from Grand Traverse veterans transport coverage; Norman historical refuge statements differ from Leon County registration/screening; Ohio charity credential interpretation differs from Missouri volunteer coordination. The fact-checking tasks respectively inspect enrollment populations, Census revisions, fire-response counting units and water-planning forecasts. None asserts that its locality or source is defective.

Candidates dropped or replaced during review:

- Additional cooling-center hours and elevator-alert checks overlapped the existing Phoenix and Chicago tasks.
- Another museum sensory-support comparison overlapped Smithsonian coverage; Arkansas now compares device demonstration, loans and reuse.
- A New York water-use comparison repeated the new San Marcos water-planning subject; Ithaca now checks fire-response counting units.
- Generic Virginia map-portal metadata was too close to existing GIS reuse tasks; Roanoke now targets raster missing values, vertical units and acquisition metadata.
- A Mississippi IIIF-specific checker lacked a confirmed local interface; the final task uses MDL’s published metadata guidelines and template.
- Search results for Manchester, New Hampshire, Warren County, Tennessee, and Lafayette, Colorado/Indiana were excluded from the Connecticut, Kentucky and Louisiana briefs. East Baton Rouge supplies a confirmed official proctoring policy.

## Source and deployment boundaries

Exact starting URLs are recorded in `lib/nationwide-task-briefs.ts` and become task inputs and source expectations. Sources were discovered in official agency, provider, university, archive or standards documentation; some pages are archived, dynamic or deny automated retrieval. The briefs explicitly require current editions, access-limit reporting and corroboration before conclusions. An inaccessible document is not treated as evidence of a local problem. A direct 92-URL audit initially returned 78 successful responses, 11 automated-access denials and three unavailable older hosts. The unavailable Greenville and UVM links were replaced with verified current official destinations; Meridian was also moved to its canonical city URL. Access-denied sources remain publicly indexed and the briefs require contributors to record retrieval limits.

The existing curated release mechanism validates each new task against the current creation schema, inserts approved non-demo Mission Desk work atomically, and records fixed task/release events. Conflicts never overwrite existing rows; retrying or losing a completion marker cannot rewrite claims, moderation, handoffs or history. No schema or hosting changes are needed. The publication manifest includes the new public definitions and this review through its existing exact-path boundary.

Production follows a focused branch and PR in the private operational repository, then the existing main-branch Cloudflare build. Verification must check all 52 IDs, approved/open state, category distribution, five-minute budgets and task-board discoverability. The expected open count is **222** if the 170-task baseline does not change through normal user activity.

IndexNow limitation: the existing integration intentionally excludes internal seeds and read requests. This release does not change that behavior or send a manual indexing batch. New approved tasks remain eligible for the existing sitemap, and subsequent supported public edits can trigger normal notifications. Do not report that this seed release automatically notified Bing.
